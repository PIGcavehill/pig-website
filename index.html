The future of healthcare starts with compassion.

The Paediatric Interest Group (PIG) is a student-led community at UWI Cave Hill building clinical skill and heart for child health — through hands-on training, workshops, and outreach that puts children first.

How to get involved

i.

Follow & sign up

Follow @pigcavehill on Instagram and fill out our sign-up form to join the mailing list.

ii.

Attend a session

Come to a workshop, camp, or outreach event — no paediatrics experience required.

iii.

Get involved further

Volunteer at outreach events or step into a leadership role on the executive board.

Who can join?

All new medical students at UWI Cave Hill — no experience needed, just a big heart and a willingness to learn.
